## Introduction

Voice and speech are low-cost, repeatable signals that may encode information about aging, neurological function, respiratory physiology, mental health, laryngeal function, and cardiometabolic state. The field is fragmented across clinical domains, elicitation tasks, languages, model families, and validation standards. This review maps the evidence base across populations and tasks and evaluates the maturity of voice-derived biomarkers for research and clinical translation.
