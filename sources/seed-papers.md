# Seed Papers

Date checked: 2026-06-05

## 1. VoxKnesset

Citation status: preprint.

- Title: VoxKnesset: A Large-Scale Longitudinal Hebrew Speech Dataset for Aging Speaker Modeling
- Authors: Yanir Marmor, Arad Zulti, David Krongauz, Adam Gabet, Yoad Snapir, Yair Lifshitz, Eran Segal
- Source: arXiv
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01270
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.01270
- Submitted: 2026-03-01
- Latest version checked: v2, 2026-03-05
- Population/data: Hebrew parliamentary speech, 393 speakers, 2009-2025, about 2,300 hours.
- Tasks: age prediction; longitudinal speaker verification.
- Models/representations: WavLM-Large, ECAPA-TDNN, Wav2Vec2-XLSR-1B.
- Key seed relevance: longitudinal voice change; age signal; speaker identity drift; language-specific dataset infrastructure.
- Evidence status: working/tentative until peer-reviewed.

## 2. Sound of Aging

Citation status: preprint.

- Title: Sound of Aging: Large-Scale Evidence for a Voice-Based Biological Clock
- Source: medRxiv
- URL: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.05.26350190v1.full-text
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.05.26350190
- Posted: 2026-04-06
- Population/data: 7,081 adults aged 40-70 with 30-second voice recordings.
- Task: voice-predicted age / voice age.
- Key seed relevance: biological aging clock from voice; associations with adiposity, sleep physiology, and cardiometabolic risk markers.
- Evidence status: working/tentative until peer-reviewed.
